What is Palladium?

Bitcoin's consensus, one fifth of the wait.

Palladium inherits Bitcoin's cryptographic foundation — SHA-256 proof-of-work, a fixed supply, and a predictable halving schedule — while cutting block time from 10 minutes to 2. The result is a chain that is faster to confirm, easier to mine, and free from central control.

What you get

Bitcoin's security model with a faster clock.

  • 01

    Bitcoin's proof-of-work

    Same SHA-256 hashing as Bitcoin, so the security model is the one already tested on the largest PoW chain.

  • 02

    2-minute blocks

    Bitcoin targets 10 minutes. Palladium targets 2, so confirmations arrive five times sooner.

  • 03

    ASIC compatible

    Existing SHA-256 ASICs mine Palladium as they are. No new hardware.

  • 04

    Fixed supply

    Capped at 21,000,000 PLM with a halving every 210,000 blocks — a schedule fixed in the code.

  • 05

    Solo mining pool

    Poolladium, built on ckpool, pays the full block reward to your address when you find a block.

  • 06

    Open source

    Core, wallet, pool and explorer are public and auditable on GitHub.
